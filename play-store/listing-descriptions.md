## Full description (4000 chars max)
Doc Scanner & Vectorizer helps you capture paper documents, correct their edges, clean up the image, and export the result as a sharp scan or portable SVG vector.

Use it for receipts, notes, forms, handwritten sketches, whiteboard captures, and other everyday pages that need a clear digital copy.

Features:
- Live camera boundary tracking for document alignment
- Manual capture when you want full control
- Perspective correction for cropped paper scans
- Paper filters including Original, Grayscale, Monochrome, Shadow Removed, and Magic Color
- Notes and keywords for saved scans
- Recent scan history stored on the device
- PNG sharing for clean document copies
- SVG vector export for portable outlines and simplified scanned shapes
- Gallery import for existing images

Your saved scans and notes are kept in the app's local storage unless you choose to share or export them.

Capture the page, choose the cleanup style, add a note, and keep both a readable scan and a vector-friendly copy ready to send.
